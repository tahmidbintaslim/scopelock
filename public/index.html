How it works

  1. List what's in scope, the UAT checks, and what's out of scope. Paste it from your SOW or write it in the app.
  2. Send the link. The client accepts or rejects each item, comments on rejections, acknowledges the exclusions, and signs.
  3. Keep the receipt. It's an HMAC-signed record of what was shown, who signed, and when. Change one character and verification fails.
  4. Handle "one more thing" with a change request. Start from the signed receipt. The client sees a diff against the baseline they approved: added, changed and removed items.

FAQ

Is this a legally binding e-signature?
It's a clear, tamper-evident record of what the client approved and when. Use it alongside your contract, not instead of a regulated e-signature such as DocuSign. Signer identity is self-declared.

Where is my data?
On the free tier, inside the link. Our server signs it and keeps no copy. Keep your receipt link somewhere safe.
