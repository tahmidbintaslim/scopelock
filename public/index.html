How it works

  1. List what is in scope, the UAT checks and what is out of scope. Paste a list and each line becomes an item.
  2. Send the link. The client accepts or rejects each item and must comment on any rejection. They acknowledge the exclusions and sign.
  3. Keep the receipt. It records the document, the decisions, the signer's name and email as entered, and the time, signed with HMAC-SHA256. Changing one character makes verification fail.
  4. Start a change request from a signed receipt for any new ask. The client sees added, changed and removed items compared with what they approved.

FAQ

Is this a legally binding e-signature?
No. It is a signed record of what the client approved and when. Use it next to your contract, not instead of a regulated e-signature service. The signer's identity is self-declared.

Where is my data?
On the free tier, inside the link. The server signs it and keeps no copy, so save your receipt link somewhere safe.
