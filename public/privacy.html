Privacy

On the free tier, ScopeLock stores no documents, signatures or receipts. Each one is encoded in the link and signed by the server, which keeps no copy. The data sits in the URL fragment (after the #), and browsers do not send that part in requests.

I store waitlist emails, plus the plan and note you give me, so I can contact you about ScopeLock. To rate-limit sign-ups, the waitlist form also keeps a keyed hash of your IP address, never the address itself. Stripe processes payments.

When a client signs, the receipt records the name and email they enter, the time, their country (from the IP address) and a truncated hash of their browser's user agent. It does not store the IP address.
