Privacy

On the free tier, ScopeLock stores no documents, signatures or receipts. Each one is encoded in the link and signed by our server, which keeps no copy. Links put the data in the URL fragment (after the #), and browsers don't send that part in requests.

We store waitlist emails, plus the plan and note you give us, so we can contact you about ScopeLock. We use Vercel Web Analytics, which is cookieless and collects aggregate page views. Payments are processed by Stripe.

When a client signs, the receipt records the name and email they enter, the time, their country (from the IP address) and a truncated hash of their browser's user agent. It never stores the IP address itself.
